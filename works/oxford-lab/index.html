Questions

Can I study online?

Yes. Lessons run online and in our Batumi classroom.

I'm a complete beginner. Where do I start?

English for Beginners: basic words, simple grammar, first conversations.

How long is a course?

Most programmes take 3 months. Entrance exam preparation takes 7.

Private or group?

Both. Tell us your goal and we will suggest the format.

Which exams do you prepare for?

IELTS, TOEFL, national entrance exams and the teacher's exam.

What do I need for online lessons?

A phone, tablet or laptop, stable internet, headphones, a notebook and a pen.
