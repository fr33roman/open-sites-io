About the tour

This journey reveals Georgia in all its variety, from the Black Sea coast to the high-mountain regions of the Caucasus. The route brings together nature, history and the spirit of Georgian culture.

The road crosses mountain passes, old towns and small villages where the traditional way of life is still alive. The trip is made not only of beautiful places but also of meeting people, conversations, the flavors of Georgian food and that famous hospitality people come back for.

  • The trip lasts 8 days and 7 nights
  • Can start from Batumi or Kutaisi

Itinerary

Day 1Batumi

We meet you at Batumi airport and transfer you to your hotel. After check-in and a short rest, an evening walking tour of the city begins. It’s your first introduction to Batumi and its main sights: the Black Sea promenade, the Old Town, the squares, the architecture and the lights of the resort at night.

Day 2Mountain Adjara

The first half of the day is free. Spend time by the sea, relax on the beach or take an easy stroll around Batumi.

In the afternoon we head into Mountain Adjara. Life here moves at a calm pace, very different from resort Batumi. Along the way your guide shares stories about the region, its traditions and the life of local families. The drive winds through mountain landscapes, introduces you to the real Adjarian character and includes a tasting of 10 kinds of Georgian wine.

Day 3Svaneti

Today we leave Batumi for Svaneti, one of Georgia’s most impressive mountain regions. Along the way you’ll see mountains, rivers and gorges, and stops at beautiful spots let you really take in the journey.

By evening we reach Mestia. We walk among the Svan towers and visit a traditional Svan house museum, where you can see how people lived in these mountains for centuries and how their daily life was arranged. We go up to a viewpoint with swings, which opens onto panoramic views of the Svan mountains.

Day 4Kutaisi

On day four we head to the high-mountain village of Ushguli, one of the highest permanently inhabited settlements in Europe. The road winds through the ridges of the Caucasus, with views of snowy peaks along the way.

In Ushguli we walk among the ancient Svan towers and soak up the atmosphere of this unique place. Nearby rises Mount Shkhara, 5,201 meters high and the tallest peak in Georgia, creating one of the most impressive landscapes of the whole trip. After the walk we drive to Kutaisi over Zagaro Pass, where the road runs through high-mountain panoramas.

Day 5Borjomi

Today we continue our journey from Kutaisi to Borjomi. The day brings together ancient monasteries, nature and one of Georgia’s most famous resorts.

In Borjomi we stroll through the green park, taste mineral water from the spring and walk up to the thermal pools. In the evening we arrive in Akhaltsikhe and end the day with a walk around Rabati Castle, lit up at night.

Day 6Goderdzi Pass

Today we return to Batumi over Goderdzi Pass. The road winds through mountains, forests and alpine scenery considered some of the most beautiful in Georgia.

On the way we stop at an old monastery, a mountain lake and natural sights. The day passes amid mountain nature and gradually brings us back to the Black Sea coast.

Day 7Free day

A free day in Batumi. Spend time by the sea, stroll along the promenade, sit in an Old Town café, drop by Boni Market for Georgian sweets, spices and tea, or simply rest after the busy days.

Everyone spends this day at their own pace, soaking up the atmosphere of a Black Sea resort town.

If you’re flying out of Tbilisi, a first-class Batumi–Tbilisi train is included. On arrival in Tbilisi — transfer to your hotel and free time in the city.

Day 8Departure

Breakfast at the hotel and free time before departure. Transfer to the airport and the end of your journey through Georgia.

We say goodbye to our guests and hope this trip stays with you as beautiful views, the warmth of Georgian hospitality and moments you’ll want to live again.
