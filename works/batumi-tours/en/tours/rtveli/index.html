About the tour

Rtveli is Georgia’s biggest wine festival, the harvest season when winemaking families begin a new wine year. For guests it’s a rare chance to see the tradition from the inside and become part of a real Georgian celebration.

Guests pick grapes, take part in the age-old ritual of stomping grapes by foot, taste fresh grape juice and spend the day surrounded by music, toasts and a Georgian feast.

  • Departure at 10:00
  • Tour duration: 8 hours

Itinerary

  1. Grape picking in the vineyard
  2. A traditional wine press (where we put the harvested grapes)
  3. Treading the grapes by foot (an old Georgian ritual, to Celentano’s music)
  4. Tasting fresh grape juice
  5. A festive lunch with a Georgian family

What’s included

  • Certified guide
  • Comfortable transfer
  • Grape picking
  • Festive lunch
  • Alcoholic and soft drinks in the car
