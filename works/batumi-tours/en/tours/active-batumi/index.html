About the journey

This route runs through Batumi, Mountain Adjara, Kutaisi, Borjomi and Tbilisi, bringing together the country’s most striking places. In a short time you’ll see canyons, mountain roads, ancient monasteries, natural springs and cities, each with its own atmosphere.

This is an active, intensive journey with a packed program, where every day is full of drives, new places and new impressions. The tour suits those who want to see as much of Georgia as possible in a short time and are ready for a fast-paced trip.

  • The trip lasts 5 days and 4 nights
  • Can start from Batumi or Kutaisi

Itinerary

Day 1BATUMI

We meet you at Batumi airport and transfer you to your hotel. After check-in and a short rest, an evening walking tour of the city begins. It’s your first introduction to Batumi and its main sights: the Black Sea promenade, the Old Town, the squares, the architecture and the lights of the resort at night.

Day 2Kutaisi

One of the most active days of the trip, with plenty of locations, walks and constantly changing scenery. In the morning we head toward Mountain Adjara, getting to know the region on the move, with stories, atmosphere and plenty of local color.

In the second half of the day the route continues with natural sights. The Martvili and Okatse canyons give you a sense of scale and the true power of nature, and the day ends with dinner with a Georgian family, with traditional food and warm hospitality.

Day 3Borjomi

The day moves at an active pace, with lots of locations and constantly changing impressions. In the morning the route starts with the historic sites of Kutaisi, after which we head to Borjomi, where the day continues amid nature and mountain air.

In Borjomi the rhythm and feel of the route change: forest, mountain air and natural springs. If you like, we take a forest trail with an open-air swim. In the second half of the day we continue on, and the shift from nature to architecture and history is especially striking when the route brings us to Rabati Fortress.

Day 4Tbilisi

The day takes in key historic sites of Georgia: the ancient cave city of Uplistsikhe, with a history of more than 3,000 years, and Mtskheta, one of the country’s oldest capitals, where you can truly feel its depth and history.

In the second half of the day we arrive in Tbilisi and continue on foot: the Old Town, a climb to panoramic viewpoints and an evening walk through the historic districts.

Day 5Departure

Breakfast at the hotel and free time before departure. Transfer to the airport and the end of your journey through Georgia.

We say goodbye to our guests and hope this trip stays with you as beautiful views, the warmth of Georgian hospitality and moments you’ll want to live again.

*If you decide to stay in Tbilisi for a few more days, you can extend your trip and spend more time in the city; we’ll arrange your airport transfer at a time that suits you. If needed, we’ll also help you find and book a hotel.
