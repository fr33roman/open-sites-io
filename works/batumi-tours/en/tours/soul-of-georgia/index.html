About the tour

This route runs through historic cities and is built around the country’s cultural and architectural heritage. Throughout the trip a certified guide shares its history and key events, helping you understand Georgia and how it developed more deeply.

The program includes monasteries, churches and cave complexes that preserve the architecture of different eras, where you can feel how the country’s history took shape. Ending by the sea in Batumi gives you time to unwind and take it all in after a packed route.

  • The trip lasts 8 days and 7 nights
  • Starts in Tbilisi

Itinerary

DAY 1 — TBILISI

We meet you at Tbilisi airport and transfer you to your hotel. After check-in and a short rest, an evening walking tour of the city begins. It’s your first introduction to Tbilisi and its main sights.

  1. Meet and greet at Tbilisi airport
  2. Lunch
  3. Transfer to the hotel
  4. Check-in and a short rest
  5. EVENING WALKING TOUR OF TBILISI
  6. Old Tbilisi and a walk through its historic streets
  7. Narikala Fortress with a panoramic view of the city
  8. Mother of Georgia
  9. Cable car over Tbilisi
  10. Abanotubani district (the famous sulfur baths)
  11. Rike Park
  12. Bridge of Peace
  13. Shardeni district
  14. Sioni Cathedral (5th–6th century)
  15. Anchiskhati Church (one of the city’s oldest churches)
  16. Return to the hotel

DAY 2 — MTSKHETA

The day takes in key historic sites of Georgia and gives you a deeper understanding of the country. This is where you begin to discover its ancient history, from the first Christian shrines to cave cities carved into the rock more than 3,000 years ago. Gradually the route leads away from the capital to calmer, greener regions.

  1. 08.00breakfast
  2. 08.30departure for Akhaltsikhe
  3. Jvari Monastery (UNESCO site)
  4. Mtskheta (Georgia’s ancient capital)
  5. Svetitskhoveli Cathedral
  6. Lunch
  7. Uplistsikhe cave city
  8. The town of Gori
  9. Joseph Stalin Museum (optional)
  10. Arrival in Akhaltsikhe
  11. Dinner
  12. Hotel check-in

DAY 3 — BORJOMI

The day moves at a calmer pace, combining history and nature. In the morning we visit Rabati Fortress, one of the most important historic sites in the region. Then the route heads into the green part of Georgia, where among the forests and mountains you can not only see Borjomi but also taste mineral water straight from the spring and bathe in the thermal waters.

  1. 08.30breakfast
  2. 09.00departure for Kutaisi
  3. Rabati Fortress
  4. Green Monastery
  5. Arrival in Borjomi
  6. Lunch
  7. Monastery of Seraphim of Sarov
  8. Borjomi Central Park
  9. Mineral springs
  10. Forest walk (optional)
  11. Bathing in the thermal springs
  12. Arrival in Kutaisi
  13. Dinner
  14. Hotel check-in

DAY 4 — KUTAISI

The day is spent among the natural sights around Kutaisi and brings some of the most striking visual impressions of the whole trip. First we visit Prometheus Cave, with its vast halls, stalactites and stalagmites that feel like an underground world.

At midday, lunch with barbecue at a Georgian family café, an important part of the route where you get the real atmosphere and taste of Georgian food. Then a walk through Okatse Canyon, with its hanging trail and panoramic views over the gorge.

  1. 08.00breakfast
  2. 08.30departure
  3. Bagrati Cathedral (UNESCO site)
  4. Prometheus Cave (underground halls with stalactites and underground lakes)
  5. Lunch at a family-run café
  6. Okatse Canyon (a panoramic trail above the gorge)
  7. Return to the hotel in Kutaisi
  8. Dinner
  9. Free time

DAY 5 — KUTAISI

The day combines nature, the road and a gradual descent toward the sea. Along the way come green landscapes, canyons and a warmer climate, and the route itself grows lighter and more relaxed.

The highlight of the day is Martvili Canyon, with its turquoise water and a walk through the gorge. Then thermal springs, a river swim (in season) and a stop at Ureki’s magnetic beach, followed by arrival in Batumi.

  1. 08.00breakfast
  2. 08.30departure for Batumi
  3. Martvili Monastery (7th century)
  4. Martvili Canyon
  5. Thermal springs
  6. Swimming in a mountain river (in season)
  7. Lunch at a family-run café
  8. Ureki Beach (magnetic sand)
  9. Eucalyptus park
  10. Arrival in Batumi
  11. Hotel check-in in Batumi
  12. Dinner

DAY 6 — MOUNTAIN ADJARA

The first half of the day is free. Spend time by the sea, relax on the beach or take an easy stroll around Batumi.

In the afternoon we head into Mountain Adjara. Life here moves at a calm pace, very different from resort Batumi. Along the way your guide shares stories about the region, its traditions and the life of local families. The drive winds through mountain landscapes and introduces you to the real Adjarian character.

  1. Free time by the sea
  2. 14.00departure from Batumi
  3. The meeting of two rivers (panoramic mountain view)
  4. The “Waterfall of Love”
  5. Makhuntseti Waterfall
  6. Kapnistavi village
  7. Ethnographic museum (open-air museum)
  8. Kapnistavi waterfalls
  9. Wine tasting (10 kinds of wine and 1 kind of chacha)
  10. Viewpoint over Gvara Fortress
  11. 1,100 m zipline (optional)
  12. The “Machakhela” rifle monument
  13. Queen Tamar Bridge (a 12th-century stone bridge)
  14. Swimming in a mountain river (in season)
  15. Return to Batumi (around 18:00)
  16. Free time

DAY 7 — FREE DAY

A free day in Batumi. Spend time by the sea, stroll along the promenade, sit in an Old Town café, drop by Boni Market for Georgian sweets, spices and tea, or simply rest after the busy days.

Everyone spends this day at their own pace, soaking up the atmosphere of a Black Sea resort town.

If you’re flying out of Tbilisi, a first-class Batumi–Tbilisi train is included. On arrival in Tbilisi — transfer to your hotel and free time in the city.

DAY 8 — DEPARTURE

Breakfast at the hotel and free time before departure. Transfer to the airport and the end of your journey through Georgia.

We say goodbye to our guests and hope this trip stays with you as beautiful views, the warmth of Georgian hospitality and moments you’ll want to live again.
