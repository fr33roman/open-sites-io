    Frequently asked questions

    How do I book a tour?

    Reach us any way you like: VK, Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram or by phone. We reply quickly, answer your questions and help you pick the right Georgia tour as soon as possible.

    Is Georgia safe to visit?

    Yes, Georgia is considered a safe country to travel in, with low crime and famously warm hospitality. In tourist cities such as Batumi and Tbilisi you’ll feel comfortable both during the day and in the evening, and locals treat guests with respect and attention. That’s why so many travelers choose Georgia for their holidays and keep coming back.

    When is the best time to go?

    You can visit Georgia at any time of year. Summer means the sea and beach days in Batumi, winter means ski resorts such as Gudauri, and spring and autumn are for nature, mountains, wine and food tours. Batumi has also won a World Travel Award as one of Europe’s best year-round destinations — proof that Georgia is a place to travel all year long.

    Do I need a visa for Georgia?

    Most nationalities don’t need a visa for Georgia. Citizens of many countries can stay for up to 365 days visa-free, which makes the trip as simple and easy as it gets. Before you travel, we recommend checking the current entry rules for your citizenship.

    Do I need travel insurance?

    Yes, travel insurance is mandatory for Georgia and must cover your entire stay. It protects you from unexpected costs and gives you peace of mind on the road. Message us and we’ll send you a link to trusted partners where you can quickly buy the right policy.

    Why choose us?

    People choose us for the experience behind every route. More than 12 years in tourism let us plan trips that are not only packed with things to see, but also comfortable and thought through to the last detail. We pay attention to safety, logistics and the pace of the journey so that every day runs without overload or unnecessary rush. Our tours are run by trusted guides and drivers who know the roads well, mountain serpentines included, and can plan the route with every nuance in mind. These are not random people but a team that understands how to make a trip calm, safe and truly high-quality. We don’t just show you places — we create a complete experience in which everything works as one system.

    Who leads the tours?

    Our tours are led by certified local guides with professional training in tourism. So you get more than a trip — you get a real immersion in Georgian culture and traditions, with lively storytelling, fascinating stories and attention to detail. Tours are run in Russian and English.

    What’s included in the price?

    What’s included depends on the route you choose. We have more than 30 tours, and each of them comes with its own terms: some include meals, others tastings, master classes or extra activities. Full details of what’s included and what costs extra are given in the description of each tour, so you can understand the format of the trip in advance and choose the option that suits you best.
