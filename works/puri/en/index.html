    How we bake

    • Sourdough starter, not packet yeast

      It’s four years old. That’s where the tangy crust and dense crumb come from.

    • Eighteen hours of proofing at 18 °C

      So the bread is still fresh in the evening.

    • Stone oven, 250 °C

      Hence the thick crust and the crack on top. It’s not a flaw — it means the dough was ready.

    • We bake at night

      At six in the morning it’s on the counter; by seven the first boxes are on their way.

    • What doesn’t sell by evening goes for half price

      There’s never yesterday’s bread on the counter — except the almond croissant, that’s the recipe.
